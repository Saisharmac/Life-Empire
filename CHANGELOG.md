# Changelog

## v0.7.0 — Expanded life, education, careers and event catalogue

- Added a structured content catalogue loaded before the game engine: 4 school types, 7 streams, 37 exams, 55 programmes, 80 institutions, 22 career tracks, 119 job roles and 170 events.
- Added institution/programme matching, entrance-route preparation and exam-result-driven admissions flow.
- Expanded job role requirements with age, education, field skills, experience, portfolio/evidence and progression links.
- Added age-banded weighted events with cooldowns, multiple choices and consequence data.
- Tightened business-capital accounting: owner injections are equity, not revenue; formation expenses are recognised; upgrades are capital expenditure; owner distributions require realised retained earnings and operating liquidity.
- Added adjusted-net-asset company book values and retained-earnings distribution safeguards.
- Retained the compact fixed-screen PWA interface and versioned local save model.
- Added catalogue integrity validation and official reference starting points in `SOURCES.md`.
- Updated service-worker cache namespace to v0.7.0.

Earlier v0.2/v0.3 milestones introduced birth-to-legacy play, three decisions per quarter, the compact HUD, economic reporting, business operations and player-owned banks. See the previous release documentation for historical details.
