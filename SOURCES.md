# Reference sources and simulation-data policy

The catalogue is a game abstraction. These official sources are starting points for checking current real-world rules; they are not embedded as live dependencies, and admission rules, fees, dates and eligibility can change each cycle.

## Content policy
- Institution presence in the game does not imply a current affiliation, course offering, seat, admission guarantee or official partnership.
- Fees, salaries, exam difficulty, thresholds and probabilities are illustrative balancing values unless explicitly labelled otherwise (the current catalogue treats them as simulation estimates).
- A job's alternate skill/portfolio route is only a gameplay route for roles where employers may assess demonstrated capability. It must not bypass legally required degrees, registration, licences, supervised training or examinations for regulated professions.
- Game market prices, dividends, yields, loan rates and exchange rates are fictional and must never be presented as current financial data.
